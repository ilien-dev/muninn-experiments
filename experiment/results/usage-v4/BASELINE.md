## Reading notes

- C1 counts every rendered block, including `commit_linked` blocks whose header carries the session `git`; the analysis parser skipped those, which is why its count of returned records is lower while the labelled figures are the same.
- B1: an item missing from a replay arm is `absent` (its transcript is no longer on disk, or the arm never typed it); usage-v1 counts those as not measured. Compare arms, not this table with usage-v1's.
- E was run on a working machine; process start alone moves between runs. Read E only from interleaved runs of two builds (`--against`), never against these absolute figures.
- B and the fixed tests of C3 and C5 are not split; their figures sit under `all`.
- Binding readings are those of the third amendment; `verdict.py <base> <candidate>` applies them.
